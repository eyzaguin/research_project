The brief

From raw data
to research.

Throughout the semester, one real-world Kaggle dataset becomes the foundation for a complete analytical story.

Rather than twelve unrelated assignments, each stage builds on the last: framing a question, preparing evidence, making sense of patterns, and communicating findings with clarity and care.

15project learning goals, one reproducible workflow

Learning goals

Skills that
compound.

  1. Work with real-world Kaggle datasets.
  2. Formulate meaningful research questions.
  3. Prepare and clean structured data.
  4. Perform basic SQL analysis.
  5. Conduct exploratory data analysis.
  6. Select appropriate visualization methods.
  7. Create publication-quality visualizations.
  8. Develop interactive visualizations.
  9. Design analytical dashboards.
  10. Communicate findings through data storytelling.
  11. Evaluate ethics, privacy, access, and fairness.
  12. Participate in a Kaggle competition.
  13. Build a reproducible analytical workflow.
  14. Communicate findings in research-paper format.
  15. Deliver a complete data visualization project.
