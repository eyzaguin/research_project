A0112-assignment project guide

Research question
& project proposal

Exploring the relationship between musical characteristics and song popularity on Spotify.

Research topic

What makes a song
popular?

This project investigates whether measurable audio characteristics are associated with how popular a song becomes on Spotify. It focuses on patterns in the music itself while recognizing that popularity is also shaped by artists, marketing, trends, and exposure.

Nicole Eyzaguirre
TECH*3745*01 · Professor Xiwang Guo

01

Dataset

Spotify Tracks Dataset
| Audio Features

I selected the Spotify Tracks Dataset | Audio Features on Kaggle. It contains approximately 114,000 songs across 114 music genres, including Spotify popularity scores and audio features such as danceability, energy, tempo, duration, valence, loudness, acousticness, and speechiness.

02

Problem & purpose

Popularity varies.
The reasons are complex.

Thousands of songs are available through streaming platforms, but they receive very different levels of attention. This project asks whether the actual musical characteristics of songs can help explain those differences.

Understanding these relationships could provide insight for artists, producers, record labels, streaming platforms, and researchers interested in music analytics. The results will describe associations, not prove that any single characteristic causes popularity.

Primary research question

What musical characteristics are associated with higher song popularity on Spotify?

Sub-questions

  1. Is danceability associated with higher song popularity?
  2. Is a song's energy level related to its popularity?
  3. Does popularity differ across music genres?
  4. Is song tempo related to popularity?
  5. Is song duration associated with popularity?
03

Methodology

A measured path from
dataset to evidence.

The analysis will use a reproducible, quantitative workflow to examine relationships in the selected Kaggle dataset.

01

Prepare the data

Inspect the schema, remove duplicate tracks where appropriate, assess missing values, check variable types, and document all cleaning decisions.

02

Describe the sample

Summarize popularity and audio features with counts, ranges, means, medians, and distributions. Examine genre balance before comparing groups.

03

Compare relationships

Use scatterplots and correlation measures to examine popularity with danceability, energy, tempo, duration, and other continuous features.

04

Compare genres

Use grouped summaries and visual comparisons to evaluate how average popularity and audio profiles vary across track genres.

05

Model and test

Use a multiple regression model, with popularity as the outcome, to estimate the association of selected audio features while accounting for genre where possible.

06

Check uncertainty

Report effect sizes, confidence intervals, and practical limitations alongside statistical significance. Check whether outliers or uneven genre sizes change the interpretation.

04

Variables & hypotheses

What will be
measured?

Dependent variable

popularity, a Spotify score from 0 to 100.

Independent variables

danceability, energy, tempo, duration_ms, and track_genre.

Additional variables

valence, loudness, acousticness, speechiness, explicit, and instrumentalness.

Preliminary hypotheses

H1 Songs with higher danceability scores will tend to have higher popularity scores.

H2 Average song popularity will differ across music genres.

H3 Songs with higher energy levels will tend to have higher popularity scores.

Scope note

Association is not
causation.

Spotify popularity may also reflect marketing, artist recognition, social media trends, playlist placement, release timing, and other factors not included in this dataset. The final analysis will treat these results as evidence of relationships within the sample, not a formula for making a song popular.
